## Research Program

My current research is organized around the broader question:

> **How do organizations build, govern, and convert AI capabilities into reliable human–AI collaboration, innovation, and business value?**

### Macro / Organization Level

External AI Knowledge → Absorptive Capacity → Enterprise AI Capability → Innovation Capability → Organizational Performance

AI Governance is studied as an enabling and boundary condition.

### Micro / Human–AI Level

Explainability → Understanding → Trust Calibration → Appropriate Reliance → Human–AI Collaboration → Decision Quality

A related stream studies **Risk-Adaptive Delegation**: how decision authority should be allocated among autonomous action, notification, and human approval depending on action risk and time criticality.

## Research Repository Principles

For research repositories, I try to distinguish:

Source Evidence → Observed Digital Trace → Derived Analytical Feature → Theoretical Construct → Model / Inference

This includes explicit attention to provenance, source coverage, entity resolution, temporal leakage, missingness vs. observed zero, reproducibility, licensing, and construct validity.
